# Pocket Pact

A social savings mobile application designed for college students to achieve their financial goals together.

## Project Overview

Pocket Pact is a Flutter-based mobile application that transforms individual saving into a collaborative and engaging social experience. By combining the power of peer accountability with shared financial goals, Pocket Pact makes saving money more motivating and achievable for college students.

## What is Pocket Pact?

Pocket Pact is a social savings app that enables friend groups to create shared savings goals called "pacts" and track their collective progress. Whether it's saving for a spring break trip, group concert tickets, or shared apartment expenses, Pocket Pact turns financial responsibility into a fun, social activity.

### Key Features:
- **Shared Savings Goals**: Create and join pacts with friends
- **Real-time Progress Tracking**: Monitor individual and group contributions
- **Social Accountability**: Stay motivated through peer engagement
- **Collaborative Planning**: Work together towards common financial objectives

## Problem It Solves

College students face unique financial challenges when it comes to saving money and coordinating group expenses. Pocket Pact makes saving a social and collaborative experience, helping students stay motivated and accountable to their financial goals.
